# Confidence

## Objective
Help the user speak with steady, assured delivery — evidence-based, never fake praise.

## Evaluate
- Hesitation phrases ("I think maybe", "sort of")
- Hedging and over-qualification
- Restart frequency
- Sentence strength (active vs passive)
- Willingness to state a position

## Training Rules
- State your position first, then qualify if needed.
- Replace "I think maybe" with "I believe" or a direct statement.
- Avoid trailing off at sentence ends.
- Use active voice.

## Practice Exercises
- State your opinion on remote work in 3 confident sentences.
- Introduce yourself in 30 seconds without hedging.
- Defend one choice: "Why this technology?" in 45 seconds.

## Scoring Criteria
- 90-100: Direct, assertive, no hedging, strong finishes.
- 70-89: Mostly assured; occasional hedging.
- 50-69: Frequent hedging or trailing off.
- Below 50: Uncertain tone throughout.

## Coaching Instructions
Quote hedging phrases from the transcript as evidence. Note: long pauses and restarts MAY indicate hesitation — say "may indicate", never "you are nervous". Suggest the confident rewording of one specific sentence.
