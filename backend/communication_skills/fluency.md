# Fluency

## Objective
Help the user speak smoothly and continuously without long pauses, restarts, or hesitation.

## Evaluate
- Pause frequency and length
- Restarts and self-corrections
- Speech rate consistency
- Connective flow between ideas
- Hesitation sounds

## Training Rules
- Keep pauses under 2 seconds.
- Use connectors (however, therefore, for example) to link ideas.
- Do not restart sentences; finish them simply.
- Practice thinking in phrases, not translated words.

## Practice Exercises
- Speak about your hometown for 60 seconds without stopping.
- Describe a photo you remember well for 45 seconds.
- Explain your morning in one continuous minute.

## Scoring Criteria
- 90-100: Continuous, natural flow; no long gaps.
- 70-89: Occasional pauses but steady rhythm.
- 50-69: Frequent restarts or long hesitations.
- Below 50: Fragmented speech; hard to follow.

## Coaching Instructions
Quote the exact moments where speech broke down (from the transcript). Count restarts and long pauses as evidence. Recommend one specific fluency drill.
