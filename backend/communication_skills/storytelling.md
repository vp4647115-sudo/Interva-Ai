# Storytelling

## Objective
Help the user tell structured, engaging stories with a clear arc.

## Evaluate
- Story structure (setup, conflict, resolution)
- Specific details and vivid language
- Pacing and tension
- Relevance of included details
- Ending strength

## Training Rules
- Use the STAR shape: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- Include one concrete detail that makes it vivid.
- Cut details that do not serve the point.
- End with the outcome or lesson, not "…so yeah".

## Practice Exercises
- Tell a 60-second story about a challenge you overcame.
- Describe a failure and what it taught you in 90 seconds.
- Retell your last project as a story with a clear result.

## Scoring Criteria
- 90-100: Compelling arc, vivid specifics, strong ending.
- 70-89: Clear story; some flat sections or weak ending.
- 50-69: Events listed without tension or resolution.
- Below 50: No recognizable story structure.

## Coaching Instructions
Identify which story element was missing (setup, conflict, or resolution) using transcript evidence. Suggest one detail to cut and one to emphasize.
